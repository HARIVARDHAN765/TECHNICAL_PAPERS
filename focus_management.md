# Focus Management

## Question 1
## What is Deep Work?

Deep Work means working with full focus on an important task without distractions.


# Question 2
## According to the author, how to do deep work properly?

* Keep phone and social media away while working.

* Focus on only one task at a time.

* Fix specific time daily for focused work.

* Rest helps the brain recover and think better.

* The more you practice focus, the better it becomes.

* Switching between tasks reduces concentration.

# Question 3
## How can you implement deep work in day-to-day life?

* Avoid notifications and social media.

* Work at the same time daily.

* Choose a quiet place.

* Complete one thing before starting another.

* Start with 30 minutes and increase slowly.

* Walk, stretch, or rest between sessions.



# Question 4
## What are the dangers of social media?

* Too much scrolling affects concentration.

* People spend many hours without realizing it.

* Constant comparison with others affects mental health.

* People feel the urge to check phones repeatedly.
 
* Using phones late at night affects sleep quality.

# Diffused Mode

Diffused mode means giving the brain time to relax and think freely.

It helps:
- creativity
- problem solving
- relaxation

# Getting Started on Difficult Tasks

Sometimes we avoid tasks because of:
- fear
- boredom
- frustration

A simple method to start:

* Work only for 30 seconds.

* Take a small break.

* Tell yourself:
 “I can do this task.”

Repeat the steps again.

This helps:
- reduce fear
- build confidence
- stop procrastination
